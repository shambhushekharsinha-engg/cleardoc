04 / Judging
What judges look for
We are looking for impactful projects that have some real-world use cases. Think about what would make your and others' lives easier.
01
Idea and Impact
The theme is open, so the problem is yours to find.
Does it solve a real problem? And what changes for the people on the other side of it?
A small problem solved well beats a big one solved vaguely.
02
Built on AWS
There are two tracks. Build It runs on the open-source stack on your own machine, and Ship It runs on AWS services, which is where the grand prize is decided. Start on the AWS Free Tier, which gives a new account up to $200 in credits, and request more from us if you need them.
Using an AWS open-source project or AWS services is mandatory to win a prize.
03
Learning
Four days should leave you knowing something you didn't on Thursday: a first deploy, a first agent, a service you had never touched.
Tell us what you learned, and it counts towards your score.
04
The execution
Does it work? Not perfect, not polished. Working. One feature that runs beats five that almost do.
05
The demo video
Three minutes, recorded, to show what it does, who it is for, and where AWS fits. There is no live demo, so the video is what the judges see.
We judge what you built, not what you spent. Local projects and deployed projects are scored with the same care. Cloud usage counts only in Ship It, where architecture and cost decisions are part of the work. Best UI is judged on design and usability.
05 / FAQ
Got questions?
What is the theme?
Build something that solves a real problem, whether it's one you deal with yourself, one people around you face every day, a service that could work better, or just a clunky way of doing things that nobody has bothered to fix yet. Think outside the box and bring creative ideas that solve the country's problems, in any sector, be that healthcare, education, finance, your pick.
Do I need to know AWS?
No. Workshops run the week before, and the Build It track needs no AWS account at all.
How do I build on AWS without paying?
Start with the AWS Free Tier. A new AWS account comes with up to $200 in free credits, on top of the services that are always free, and that is enough to build and deploy a hackathon project. Using it is completely valid for every track, Ship It included. If you need more than that, you can request credits from us.
See what the free tier covers
Where can I read up before the weekend?
We write about the tour as it goes: guides on the services you'll reach for, recaps of the stops, and build notes from the people who were there. Everything tagged aws on the blog is about this tour.
Read the blogs
Do I need a credit card?
No. Build It needs nothing at all. Ship It needs an AWS account, and the free tier makes that free: debit cards and RuPay work for the signup, and the verification charge is about ₹2.
Can I participate online?
Yes, fully. Same theme, same judging, same prizes, and no cap on numbers.
How do the fast-track interviews at Amazon work?
The judges put the top projects forward to Amazon's University Talent Acquisition Team, and those students skip the screening round: the project stands in for it. Top 10 students from top projects go forward at each event. The roles are a 6-month internship at Amazon and full-time positions. Winning a track is neither required nor enough, and the full terms are in the tour's rules.
Read the rules
Where is the venue?
Polaris School of Technology in Bangalore, open on Sept 19. Online runs the whole four days from anywhere in India.
How do I attend in person?
Two signups. Check in here first, which enters you in the hackathon online, then apply for the Bangalore build day on Luma. Seats are limited and Luma confirms yours by email.
Apply on Luma
Why would I join in person?
On Saturday there is an optional hack day in Bangalore, where you can join the workshops, get feedback on your projects, meet the Amazon team and pick up swag!
Does joining in person help my chances?
No. The hackathon runs online from Thursday to Sunday. The Bangalore day is optional and has limited seats, and being there doesn't add to your score.
Who can participate?
University students across India.
How big can a team be?
One to four people.
Is it free?
Yes. Free to register, free to take part.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes, everyone who submits a project will get a certificate, and the winning team will also get a certificate.
Can I start building before the event?
Learn and practise as much as you want. Project work starts when the clock does.
